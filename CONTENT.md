## Section 2: The Three Businesses

**Section label:** Where I'd work

**Intro line:** Hunt St runs three businesses. Each one needs a different AI layer. Here's what I'd build.

---

### Studio — Hospitality & Retail Design

**Description:** Studio designs hospitality and retail environments — All'Antico Vinaio, Racqueteer, Sea Salt Clovelly, and others. The challenge: project delivery across multiple active sites with tight timelines and builder relationships that need constant management.

**AI systems I'd build:**
- **Project Workspace Generation** — When a deal closes, a complete project environment spins up automatically: folder structure, templates, programme, kickoff brief.
- **Cost Planning Agent** — From sketch to v0 cost plan in the FOH/BOH/Services/FFE/Prelims structure, with builder benchmark lookups and version history.
- **Builder Budget Loop** — Receive revised budget, reconcile against cost plan, draft client memo, schedule next meeting. The loop runs without a human in the middle.
- **Weekly Client Status Layer** — Automated status reports per active project, pulling from drawing registers, RFI logs, and meeting notes.

---

### Table One — Hospitality Strategy & Advisory

**Description:** Table One moves operators from concept to opening — site sourcing, lease negotiation, feasibility modelling, investor decks. The challenge: high-stakes, high-judgment work that still has repeatable process buried inside it.

**AI systems I'd build:**
- **Feasibility Modelling Agent** — Brief in, financial model out. Parameterised by venue type, location, cover count, and operator history.
- **Fee Proposal Generator** — Brief captured → branded proposal drafted → version tracked → follow-up scheduled. One loop, no manual steps.
- **Pipeline Rhythm in Streak** — Deal stages updated from meeting transcripts. Proposal follow-up cadenced. The founder only sees deals that need his actual judgment.
- **Investor Deck Drafting Layer** — Structured brief → narrative arc → slide-ready content. Maintains Table One's voice without generic AI copy.

---

### No Filter — Psychology-Led Marketing Studio

**Description:** No Filter does growth and marketing for founder-led brands — brand voice, content, performance marketing, SEO. The challenge: scale content production without losing the brand voice or the creative quality that makes the work worth sharing.

**AI systems I'd build:**
- **Brand Voice Infrastructure** — Founder interviews → voice guide → enforcement layer across every piece of content output. The guide is a living document, not a PDF someone forgets.
- **Content Production Pipeline** — LinkedIn, Instagram, blog, EDM, and short-form video — all calibrated per client voice, scheduled, and variation-tested.
- **Performance Reporting Pipelines** — Meta Ads, Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads, and organic data pulled into client-ready insights. Not a dashboard, a narrative.
- **Ad Creative Variation Engine** — Creative briefs → variation sets → performance feedback loop → next creative brief. The machine learns what works.
- **SEO and AI-Search Systems** — Keyword clustering, content briefs, on-page optimisation, and schema markup. Built for GEO/AEO, not just Google.

## Section 3: The Architecture

**Section label:** How it fits together

**Headline:** One AI layer. Three businesses. Full ownership.

**Body:**
I don't build isolated tools. I build a connected operating layer — shared memory, consistent evaluation, common integrations — that compounds over time. When a system in Studio learns something about builder timelines, that knowledge can inform Table One's feasibility models. When No Filter's content performance data improves, the brand voice layer gets sharper.

**Architecture points (visual list):**
- Agents built on Claude, ChatGPT, Make, n8n, and Zapier
- Integrations across Gmail, Google Workspace, Streak, Xero, WhatsApp, WordPress, Meta Ads, Google Ads, LinkedIn, Klaviyo
- Evaluation and observability built into every agent — you know when one fails before it matters
- Memory architecture that compounds, not evaporates
- Documentation that survives a two-week holiday
